Northwind Coffee: Our Coffee

Northwind Coffee is a fictional company used for a training exercise.

Where our beans come from
We buy green coffee from small farms in three regions. Our Colombia Huila blend has notes of caramel and red apple. Our Ethiopia Guji blend is light and fruity, with notes of blueberry and jasmine. Our Guatemala Antigua blend is balanced and chocolatey, with a hint of orange. We publish the farm names for each harvest on the product pages.

Roast levels
Each coffee is roasted at one of three levels. Light roast keeps more of the bean's original fruit and floral flavors and has the highest acidity. Medium roast is balanced, sweeter, and smoother. Dark roast is bold, with smoky and bittersweet flavors and low acidity. Our Ethiopia Guji is roasted light, Colombia Huila is roasted medium, and Guatemala Antigua is roasted medium-dark.

Freshness
All of our coffee is roasted to order, within 48 hours of shipping. The roast date is printed on every bag. Coffee tastes best between 4 days and 4 weeks after the roast date. After 4 weeks, the flavor fades but the coffee is still safe to drink.

Bag sizes
Coffee is sold in 250 gram bags and 1 kilogram bags. The 1 kilogram bag costs less per gram. Whole bean and ground options are available. When you choose ground, select your brewing method at checkout and we will grind to match it.

Organic and certifications
Our Colombia Huila and Guatemala Antigua coffees are certified organic. The Ethiopia Guji is grown using organic methods, but the farm has not yet completed organic certification.

Caffeine
All of our coffees contain caffeine. We do not currently offer decaffeinated coffee.
