Northwind Coffee: Burr Grinder NG-200 Warranty

Northwind Coffee is a fictional company used for a training exercise.

What is covered
The Northwind Burr Grinder, model NG-200, comes with a limited warranty against defects in materials and workmanship. The motor is covered for 2 years from the date of purchase. The grinding burrs are covered for 1 year, because burrs naturally wear down with use.

Registering your grinder
To receive the full 2-year motor coverage, register your grinder on our website within 30 days of purchase. If you do not register within 30 days, the motor warranty is 1 year instead of 2 years. Registration requires your order number and the serial number printed on the underside of the grinder.

What is not covered
The warranty does not cover damage caused by dropping the grinder, exposing it to water or other liquids, using it to grind anything other than coffee beans, or repairs made by anyone other than Northwind. Normal wear, scratches, and cosmetic damage are not covered. The removable hopper and grounds bin are covered for 90 days only.

How to make a claim
Email support with your order number, the serial number, a short description of the problem, and a photo or short video if possible. We will respond within 1 business day. If the claim is approved, we send a prepaid shipping label for the return of the grinder.

Repair or replacement
If a covered defect is confirmed, we will repair or replace the grinder at our choice. A replacement is shipped within 10 business days after we receive the defective unit. The replacement is covered for the remainder of the original warranty period.

Cleaning
Clean the burrs every 2 to 4 weeks using a soft brush. Do not wash the burrs or any motor part with water.
